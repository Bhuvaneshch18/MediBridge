Diabetes Diet Plan

Recommended Foods

  • Leafy green vegetables (Spinach, Kale, Methi)
  • Whole grains (Oats, Quinoa, Brown rice, Ragi)
  • Plant proteins (Paneer, Tofu, Moong Dal, Chickpeas)
  • Low-glycemic fruits (Guava, Berries, Jamun, Apples)
  • Healthy fats (Almonds, Walnuts, Flaxseeds, Olive oil)

Foods to Avoid

  • Highly processed foods and fast food
  • Sugary beverages, packaged juices, and sodas
  • Refined carbohydrates (White bread, Maida, Pastries)
  • Excessive salt and sodium-rich snacks
  • Trans fats and heavily fried foods

Daily Meal Suggestions

Breakfast Suggestions

Sprouted moong dal chilla with mint chutney, or steel-cut oats with almonds and chia seeds.

Lunch Suggestions

2 Multigrain rotis, a bowl of mixed dal, steamed spinach (palak), and cucumber raita.

Dinner Suggestions

Grilled paneer or tofu tikka with sautéed broccoli, bell peppers, and a small bowl of quinoa.

Healthy Snacks

Roasted makhana (fox nuts), a handful of raw walnuts, or unsweetened buttermilk (chaas).

Hydration Tips

Drink at least 8-10 glasses of water daily. Try cinnamon or methi seed infused water in the morning.

Lifestyle Tips

Go for a 15-minute brisk walk after every meal to help regulate post-meal blood sugar levels.

Fruits & Vegetables

Aim to fill half your plate with colorful, non-starchy green vegetables at every main meal.

General Nutrition Tips

Practice Portion Control

Use smaller plates and listen to your body's hunger cues to avoid overeating. Eating slowly can also help your brain recognize when you're full.

Limit Added Sugars

Check nutrition labels for hidden sugars in sauces, dressings, and packaged foods. Opt for natural sweetness from fruits instead.
